# HITS_MAP.md — where the ten High Impact Teaching Strategies live in this suite

For Andrew (and for anyone auditing the pedagogy): the Victorian DET's ten
High Impact Teaching Strategies, and the concrete place each one is
implemented in the apps — or honestly marked "not yet". This is a map, not
a marketing document: if a row overclaims, strike it and we will fix the
app, not the row.

| # | HITS strategy | Where it lives | Honest gaps |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Setting goals** | The Unit Walk's gap check ("before you start") turns five quick checks into a personal plan with named targets; the unit page's step trail is the visible goal ladder; the report names what is and is not done. | Goals are per-unit, not per-student-over-time (no accounts, by design — Q10/privacy). |
| 2 | **Structuring lessons** | Every unit walks the same six-station road (check → words → build → talk → grammar → craft), so the structure itself is taught once and reused 25 times. Nothing is locked; the order is recommended, not enforced (DESIGN_PHILOSOPHY: never lock content). | A single unit is bigger than one lesson; Andrew may want suggested lesson-sized slices. |
| 3 | **Explicit teaching** | Capacity colours state the rule out loud (red always-meaning, blue usually-sound, purple both); every kanji build ends with a one-line hook naming WHY; grammar links carry a why-line, not just a destination; the Level Up Wall names its criteria. | — |
| 4 | **Worked examples** | Craft step: a full model answer with each craft move labelled, BEFORE the student edits anything. The Writing Wall and Level Up Wall are entire apps of worked examples. | — |
| 5 | **Collaborative learning** | Not implemented. The suite is single-player by design (no accounts, no shared state). Pair work happens off-screen: the oral player and QA steps are built to be run in pairs, but the app cannot verify that. | Genuine gap. If Andrew wants it, it is a classroom-practice note, not an app feature. |
| 6 | **Multiple exposures** | The deliberate spiral: 仕 built in Year 10 and again in VCE; 番組 met in Year 10 media and again in VCE; 寺's sound family grows across levels (時・持 → 待); the Vocab Hub shows every door a word lives behind, making the re-meeting visible; QA end screens say "ask them again tomorrow". | Spacing is suggested, not scheduled (no accounts → no SRS). |
| 7 | **Questioning** | QA step is structured questioning with self-marking; the gap check asks before teaching; edit tasks ask "which is better, and why" with the why always answered. | All questions are app-authored; no student-generated questioning. |
| 8 | **Feedback** | Immediate, specific, honest: wrong assembly shakes and clears (no fake praise), right assembly lights the actual linguistics; every edit option gets an explanation whether chosen or not; first-try scoring separates "got there" from "got there first go". | Feedback is per-item; no teacher dashboard (localStorage only, deployed builds). |
| 9 | **Metacognitive strategies** | Self-marking ○/△ in QA; "hide the help and self-test" in study; the extension checklist asks students to audit their own writing against named moves; the gap-check result is framed as a plan the student owns. | — |
| 10 | **Differentiated teaching** | The language lever (page + forgiving section levers) lets each student set their own scaffold moment to moment; readings come tiered (unit10); the Level Up Wall shows nine rungs of the same task; extension tasks sit behind the completed floor so the ceiling is open but the floor comes first; gap-check plans route each student to THEIR missing prerequisite. | Differentiation is student-driven; no teacher-assigned pathways. |

## The two structural bets

1. **Identify gaps → plan solutions** is a loop, not a report: gap check
   (identify) → deep links into the Grammar Hub shelf or Vocab Hub set
   (plan) → the unit itself (implement) → report (review). Each arrow is
   one tap.
2. **Extend and support are the same road at different speeds**, not
   different roads: support = lever + tiers + prior-unit review links;
   extend = the のばす task + checklist after the floor is walked. Nobody
   gets a "different" (lesser) unit.
