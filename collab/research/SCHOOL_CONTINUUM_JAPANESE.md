# School Learning Continuum — Japanese (extracted)

Source: the school's Learning Continuum master sheet (Languages tab), supplied by Liam
2026-08-18. Only the Japanese rows are reproduced here; the full workbook is school-internal
and stays out of the repo. Columns in the source are year levels 5-11; the Japanese rows
carry statements for years 6-11.

**This is the frame for the STUDENT-FACING year-level view**: kids see year levels and
continuum-style 'I can' statements, not curriculum band codes (Liam's decision, 2026-08-18).


## Socialising

- **Year 6:** I can engage in simple conversations about familiar topics and respond appropriately to questions and comments.
- **Year 7:** I can participate in exchanges on familiar topics and express opinions or preferences with some support.
- **Year 8:** I can initiate and sustain conversations on familiar topics, handle short social exchanges, and express personal opinions.
- **Year 9:** I can participate in more extended conversations on a range of topics, clarify and justify personal opinions and respond to the opinions of others.
- **Year 10:** I can engage in detailed conversations on a wide range of topics, manage complex social interactions, and discuss abstract ideas.
- **Year 11:** I can conduct sophisticated conversations on complex topics, discuss the pros and cons of various issues, and negotiate outcomes.

## Informing

- **Year 6:** I can write short, simple texts about familiar topics and convey meaning with support.
- **Year 7:** I can write informative texts on familiar topics, such as family, hobbies, and daily routines, using modelled language.
- **Year 8:** I can write descriptive and informative texts on a range of topics, such as past experiences and future plans.
- **Year 9:** I can write detailed informative texts on a range of topics, such as personal interests, social issues, and current events.
- **Year 10:** I can write comprehensive and nuanced informative texts on a range of complex topics, such as cultural phenomena, environmental issues, and global trends.
- **Year 11:** I can write sophisticated, well-structured informative texts that analyze and reflect upon complex concepts and ideas.

## Creating

- **Year 6:** I can create short, simple texts using familiar vocabulary and phrases.
- **Year 7:** I can create short creative texts, such as dialogues or short narratives, using familiar vocabulary and phrases.
- **Year 8:** I can create imaginative texts, such as stories or dialogues, that demonstrate a growing awareness of audience and purpose.
- **Year 9:** I can create detailed and coherent texts, such as narratives and descriptions, which show an understanding of different perspectives and cultural elements.
- **Year 10:** I can create sophisticated and imaginative texts, such as detailed narratives, persuasive arguments, and reflective compositions.
- **Year 11:** I can create refined and nuanced texts that explore complex themes, convey subtle emotions, and incorporate stylistic features appropriate to genre and audience.

## Translating

- **Year 6:** I can translate simple sentences and phrases from Japanese to English and vice versa, using basic vocabulary and grammar structures.
- **Year 7:** I can translate simple texts related to familiar topics, such as personal interests, daily routines, and school life, using appropriate vocabulary and grammar structures.
- **Year 8:** I can translate short paragraphs related to a range of topics, such as travel, food, and festivals, using a variety of vocabulary and grammar structures.
- **Year 9:** I can translate longer texts related to a range of topics, such as media, technology, and culture, using complex vocabulary and grammar structures.
- **Year 10:** I can translate a range of texts, such as literary texts, news articles, and academic papers, using a high level of accuracy and fluency.
- **Year 11:** I can translate a range of complex texts, such as historical documents, legal texts, and technical manuals, using advanced vocabulary and grammar structures, and demonstrate an understanding of cultural and linguistic nuances.

## Reflecting

- **Year 6:** I can reflect on personal experiences of using Japanese in familiar contexts and identify areas for improvement.
- **Year 7:** I can reflect on personal experiences of using Japanese in familiar contexts and identify strategies for improvement.
- **Year 8:** I can reflect on how cultural perspectives and practices influence communication in Japanese and identify ways to show respect and appreciation for them.
- **Year 9:** I can reflect on how language use varies according to audience, purpose, and context, and identify ways to adapt language accordingly.
- **Year 10:** I can reflect on how language learning strategies and techniques support language acquisition and identify ways to apply them to new learning situations.
- **Year 11:** I can analyze personal language use and identify areas for improvement, using metacognitive strategies to develop and refine language skills.

## Systems of language

- **Year 6:** I can understand and respond to simple spoken and written Japanese on familiar topics, such as daily routines, leisure activities, and family life.
- **Year 7:** I can identify key words and phrases in spoken and written texts related to familiar topics, such as daily routines, leisure activities, and family life.
- **Year 8:** I can understand the main ideas and some details in spoken and written texts related to familiar topics, such as travel, food, and festivals.
- **Year 9:** I can understand the main ideas and most details in spoken and written texts related to a range of topics, such as media, technology, and culture.
- **Year 10:** I can understand the main ideas and all details in spoken and written texts related to a range of complex topics, such as literature, history, and politics.
- **Year 11:** I can analyze and evaluate a range of spoken and written texts in Japanese, including complex literary texts and academic articles, and demonstrate critical thinking skills.

## Langauge variation and change

- **Year 6:** I can recognize some differences in how Japanese is used in different regions and social groups.
- **Year 7:** I can recognize and describe some key differences in how Japanese is used in different contexts, such as formal and informal situations.
- **Year 8:** I can explain how Japanese language use varies in different contexts, such as formal and informal situations, and among different social groups.
- **Year 9:** I can analyze and explain the reasons for language variation in Japanese across different regions, social groups, and contexts.
- **Year 10:** I can evaluate the impact of language variation on communication in Japanese and consider how language change may affect language use in the future.
- **Year 11:** I can critically analyze the ways in which language variation and change reflect cultural and social processes in Japanese-speaking communities.

## Role of Language and culture

- **Year 6:** I can recognize some cultural elements that are expressed through the Japanese language.
- **Year 7:** I can identify and describe cultural practices and perspectives that are embedded in the Japanese language.
- **Year 8:** I can explain the relationship between language and cultural identity in Japanese and discuss how language expresses cultural values.
- **Year 9:** I can analyze the interplay between language, culture, and identity in Japanese and explore how language reflects cultural practices and perspectives.
- **Year 10:** I can evaluate the role of language in maintaining and transmitting culture in Japanese-speaking communities and consider how language can influence cultural practices.
- **Year 11:** I can critically analyze and reflect on the complex relationship between language, culture, and society in Japanese-speaking communities and consider how language shapes cultural identity.
