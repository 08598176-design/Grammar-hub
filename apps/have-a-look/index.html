The bigger question

Every one of these is one tool. The thing neither of us knows is how it would actually run in your room — a composite Year 10, 11 and 12 class is a real constraint that shapes everything.

One shape Liam's been wondering about, just as a starting point, not a suggestion: could you teach one broad skill explicitly to the whole class at once — a skill that happens to link a Unit 1 topic, a Unit 3 topic and a Year 10 Semester 1 topic together — and then let the room split into differentiated pathways through the app afterwards, each year level going as deep as it actually needs? Or does your room work completely differently?

That call is genuinely yours. Talk it through with your Claude however it comes out — even half-formed — and whatever it turns into can go straight into the job queue for Liam's side to build.
